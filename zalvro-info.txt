## Services

### Web development

Zalvro builds single-page and multi-page websites, business websites, real estate listing sites, agency and portfolio sites, and web applications with custom backend functionality. The web development stack includes React, Node.js, and MongoDB (MERN).

### AI voice receptionists

Zalvro builds AI calling agents using Vapi, ElevenLabs, and Groq. They can answer or make business calls, answer frequently asked questions, book appointments, and qualify leads. They can help clinics, real estate agencies, and other businesses that receive many phone inquiries.

### AI chatbots

Zalvro builds website chatbots that answer questions, qualify leads, and guide visitors. PropBot is a real estate chatbot example built with Gemini.

### WhatsApp AI bots

Zalvro builds WhatsApp bots that automatically respond to customer messages about a business and its offerings.

### AI and workflow automation

Zalvro builds automations using n8n, Groq/Llama, and integrations such as Zapier-style workflows. Examples include lead capture and follow-up, email workflows, syncing leads to Google Sheets, and Slack notifications for new leads or replies.

### Website maintenance and improvements

Zalvro provides post-launch website maintenance, updates, bug fixes, and general upkeep. It can also redesign or improve an existing website and add features or integrations such as chatbots and automation.
